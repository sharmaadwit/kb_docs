## Segment 1: India SMBs (₹2.5K–5K/month budget, 2K–25K msgs/month)

**ICP Profile:**
- Monthly volume: 2K–25K messages
- Budget: ₹2.5K–5K/month
- Use case: OTP-heavy (60% of conversations)
- Decision-maker: Founder or ops lead (fast approval)
- Geography: India (70%+ of inbound)

### Positioning Angle

**Lead with:** "Compliance audit that prevents template rejection and approval delays"

**Why it works:** Customers are frustrated by 19-day Meta approval waits. Gupshup's audit checklist saves rework time, which is worth ₹2.5K/month fee.

**Messaging hierarchy:**

1. **Awareness:** "Know your use case first. OTP? Campaigns? Support? We'll point you to the right path."
2. **Consideration:** "19-day template approval = ₹50K+ lost business. DLT confusion = compliance risk. Gupshup solves both."
3. **Decision:** "Gupshup Starter = platform + audit + templates + support. ₹2.5K/month. Meta bills separately."
4. **Close:** "Start with Starter. Audit first 3 templates. If volume grows, upgrade to Pro."

### Objection Handling

**Objection:** "AiSensy is free. Why pay ₹2.5K?"

**Response:**
- "AiSensy is great for testing, especially if you're under 1K msgs/month. We win above 2K/month because our compliance audit saves 19 days + rework costs."
- "Think of it as audit insurance. You'll recoup ₹2.5K in avoided approval delays on your 3rd campaign."

**Objection:** "Twilio is cheaper / more flexible."

**Response:**
- "Twilio is excellent if you have an engineering team. We're built for ops teams who need templates + support, not APIs."
- "Twilio doesn't include template audit; you debug Meta's rules alone. We do that for you."

### Call to Action

- Free trial: "5-day compliance audit on first 3 templates"
- Pricing transparency: Show exact breakdown (₹2.5K access + ₹0.12 per WhatsApp OTP)
- Success metrics: "Typical SMB sees 40% faster template approval, 60% fewer rejections"

## Universal Messaging Principles

### DO:

✓ Lead with the customer's specific pain point, not Gupshup's features  
✓ Acknowledge competitor strengths ("AiSensy is genuinely free," "Twilio's dev ecosystem is superior")  
✓ Quantify ROI ("saves 19 days," "₹50K+ avoided approval delays")  
✓ Offer realistic success metrics tied to their use case  
✓ Provide an honest escape hatch ("AiSensy is better for your volume. Try them.")  

### DON'T:

❌ Claim "cheaper than competitors" (false; AiSensy is free)  
❌ Claim "only ones with compliance audit" (Twilio/others have it too)  
❌ Oversell ("Fastest approval in the industry" — Meta controls approval, not us)  
❌ Ignore the real objection (listen first, then answer)  
❌ Pretend low-volume customers are SMBs (they're not; they're evaluators)
