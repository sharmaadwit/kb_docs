# Real Estate: WhatsApp Messaging Strategy

Real estate agents and property management firms use WhatsApp for property updates, appointment confirmations, and lead follow-ups. Gupshup provides pre-built templates and compliance audit for this vertical.

## Use Cases

### 1. Property Update Broadcasts

**What:** Send property listings, price changes, or new inventory to interested buyers

**Template:** "Hi {{buyer_name}}, new property available: {{property_name}}, ₹{{price}}, {{bedrooms}} BHK. Schedule a tour: {{tour_link}}"

**Category:** Marketing (5–7 days approval)

**Typical volume:** 200–500 per agent per month

### 2. Appointment Confirmations

**What:** Confirm scheduled property tours or consultations

**Template:** "Your property tour on {{date}} at {{time}} with {{agent_name}}. Location: {{address}}. Reply to confirm or reschedule."

**Category:** Utility (2–3 days approval)

**Typical volume:** 50–100 per agent per month

### 3. Lead Follow-up

**What:** Multi-touch follow-up to warm leads (no immediate purchase intent)

**Template:** "{{lead_name}}, thanks for your interest in {{property}}. Open houses this weekend 10 AM–5 PM. Details: {{link}}"

**Category:** Marketing (5–7 days approval)

**Typical volume:** 100–200 per agent per month
