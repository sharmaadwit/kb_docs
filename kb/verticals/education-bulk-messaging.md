# Education: Bulk Student and Parent Messaging

Schools, coaching centers, and online education platforms use WhatsApp to send class updates, event reminders, and fee notifications to students and parents at scale.

## Use Cases

### 1. Class Updates & Announcements

**What:** Real-time updates about class schedule changes, holiday notices, exam dates

**Template:** "⏰ Class Alert: {{subject}} class moved to {{new_time}} today. Join Google Meet: {{meet_link}}"

**Category:** Utility (2–3 days approval)

**Throughput:** Gupshup Pro tier (80 TPS) handles 500 messages in 6 seconds

### 2. Exam Results & Report Card Notifications

**What:** Deliver exam results and report cards via WhatsApp

**Template:** "{{student_name}}, your {{exam_name}} result is ready. Score: {{score}}. View: {{result_link}}"

**Category:** Utility (2–3 days approval)

**Volume:** 1000–5000 per exam session

## Segmentation

**Setup in Gupshup:**

1. **Create segments** (Grade 10, Grade 11, Grade 12 + parents)
2. **Schedule by segment** (different message times for students vs parents)

**Example:**
- 8 AM: Class updates to students
- 4 PM: Attendance/performance summary to parents
