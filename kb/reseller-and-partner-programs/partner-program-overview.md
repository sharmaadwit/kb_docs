# Reseller and Partner Program

Gupshup's partner program is designed for agencies, consultants, and resellers who want to offer Gupshup services to their customers without managing infrastructure or billing directly.

## Program Tiers

### Agency Partner

- White-label access to Gupshup platform
- Billing pass-through (customer pays you, you pay Gupshup)
- Co-marketing and lead-sharing
- Dedicated partner success manager for 10+ customers

### Reseller Program

- Bulk account creation for 5+ customers
- Discounted per-seat cost (e.g., ₹250/customer for split Starter tier)
- API access to provision accounts programmatically
- Partner portal for managing sub-accounts

### Enterprise White-Label

- Fully branded Gupshup platform under your domain
- Custom billing and contract terms
- Dedicated support team
- Minimum 50+ seats or ₹5L+ annual commitment

## How to Apply

1. Email partners@gupshup.io with your company details, customer base size, and use case
2. 48-hour initial review; 1-week onboarding if approved
3. Compliance audit (your customers' templates)
4. Go live with shared onboarding support

## Key Benefits

- **Cost efficiency**: Split ₹2.5K Starter tier across 10 customers = ₹250/customer
- **Margin capture**: Mark up platform fees or offer as a bundled service
- **Support burden reduction**: Gupshup handles compliance audits, template approvals, DLT co-registration
- **Scalability**: Provision new customer accounts in minutes, not weeks

## Reseller Model Economics

For agencies serving 5–20 SMBs:

| Model | Customer Cost | Your Margin | Economics |
|-------|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Direct** (no bundle) | ₹2.5K/month Starter | ₹0 | Customer contacts Gupshup directly |
| **Reseller bulk** | ₹250/customer (shared seat) | ₹2.25K/month | You split Starter tier across 10 customers, keep margin |
| **White-label** | Custom pricing | Custom | You brand as your own service, add margin |

Typical reseller structure: 5–10 SMB customers paying ₹200–300/month each, rolled up to one Gupshup ₹2.5K Starter account, managed via the partner portal.
