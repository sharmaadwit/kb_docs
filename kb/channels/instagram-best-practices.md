# Instagram Best Practices (Evidence-Based)

**Module**: Channels

## Definition

These practices are derived only from Gupshup case studies that involve Instagram. Evidence is limited: most "Instagram" case studies use Instagram as an ad-placement surface that funnels users into WhatsApp, not as a native conversation channel. Only a few describe Instagram-native chatbot engagement.

## Instagram as a Native Conversation Channel

- **Build an Instagram messaging chatbot for product discovery and new-customer acquisition** — interactive chatbot conversations on Instagram DMs boost product discovery (case: cpg-3.md — Instagram messaging chatbot enabled rich interactive conversations to boost product discovery and acquire new customers; users buy via a "Buy now" button on product cards). Single-case-study evidence.
- **Engage across all Instagram entry points — DMs, comments, and story mentions — with a creative content approach** — for sensitive or brand-led categories, a chatbot that responds to DMs, comments and story mentions with tips and personalized recommendations directly connects brand to customer (case: retail-d2c-22.md — Kamasutra Instagram chatbot connected via DMs, comments, story mentions with personalized recommendations). Single-case-study evidence.
- **Run Instagram contests to collect first-party data and opt-ins** — a contest (e.g. collecting flavor suggestions) captures user info, opt-ins and emails for future campaigns, ideally via a marketing-integration (case: general-5.md — STËLZ ran an Instagram flavor-suggestion contest with Klaviyo integration to store opt-ins/emails for future campaigns). Single-case-study evidence.

## Instagram as an Ad Surface That Feeds WhatsApp

- **Use Facebook & Instagram ads to drive users into a WhatsApp conversation** — the dominant pattern across the corpus is Instagram/FB sponsored posts with a CTA to chat on WhatsApp for lead gen, bookings, and commerce (case: swiggy.md — Instagram + WhatsApp; entertainment-2.md — sponsored FB & Instagram posts prompting WhatsApp chat for event-ticket booking; real-estate-1.md — FB/Instagram ads directing to WhatsApp for attraction ticketing; cpg-1.md — FB & Instagram ads to WhatsApp chatbot across 6 countries).

## Confidence & Limitations

Instagram-native best practices rest on single-case-study evidence each (cpg-3.md, retail-d2c-22.md, general-5.md). Treat them as promising patterns, not proven playbooks. The strongest, most-repeated Instagram pattern in the corpus is simply "Instagram ad → WhatsApp conversation" — for that funnel, follow the Campaign Manager CTWA best practices (see kb/campaign-manager/campaign-best-practices.md).
