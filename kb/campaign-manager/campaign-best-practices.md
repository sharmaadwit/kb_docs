# Campaign Manager Best Practices (Evidence-Based)

**Module**: Campaign Manager

## Definition

These best practices are derived exclusively from Gupshup customer case studies. Each practice is grounded in one or more real deployments; nothing here is generic advice. Use these as proven, repeatable patterns for marketing and lead-generation campaigns on WhatsApp.

## Lead Generation via Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) Ads

- **Route social ads into a WhatsApp conversation instead of a web form** — reduces friction and improves lead quality vs. lead forms that attract dummy numbers (case: travel-hospitality-3.md — replaced lead forms with WhatsApp-chat CTA to eliminate invalid phone numbers; financial-services-9.md — CTWA generated 520+ leads "with fewer friction points compared to email & SMS campaigns").
- **Qualify leads inside the chat flow before handing to sales** — ask a short set of qualifying questions, then route only qualified prospects to agents (case: ola-electric.md — chat flow asked vehicle preference, budget, daily travel distance, financing needs; automotive-1.md — leads segmented Hot/Warm/Cold by in-chat actions; financial-services-6.md — captured number, educated, quoted, then shared qualified leads with sales for 1:1 selling).
- **Add a gamified or incentivized micro-journey to lower cost-per-lead / cost-per-download** — a fun quiz or reward mechanic improves qualification and conversion (case: retail-d2c-15.md — gamified quiz with redeemable "Snapcash" cut cost per app download to 1/10th; retail-d2c-16.md — "surprise Eid gift" hook drove 34% lower CPL and 43% lead-to-qualified conversion).
- **Set clear ad-creative expectations that the click leads to a WhatsApp chat** — a compelling creative plus explicit "chat with us on WhatsApp" CTA improves journey completion (case: entertainment-2.md — clear CTA setting expectation of WhatsApp chat yielded ~62% journey completion and 90% high-intent leads).

## First-Party Data & Consented Re-Engagement

- **Use CTWA campaigns to build a consented first-party database, then retarget for free** — the persistent WhatsApp thread lets you re-engage converted and non-converted leads without further ad spend (case: retail-d2c-14.md — "re-engaged converted and non-converted leads using the persistent chat thread"; retail-d2c-4.md — 19K+ buyers opted in to launch notifications as first-party data; cpg-1.md — captured consented first-party data across 6 markets for future campaigns).
- **Pre-launch hype campaigns to build a buyer pipeline** — collect interested users before a product launch and message them on launch day with exclusive offers (case: retail-d2c-4.md — 40% of ad users signed up for launch vs 25% from website forms).

## Segmentation & Targeting

- **Segment audiences by behavior and intent, then send tailored messages per segment** — segments proven in case studies include new users, dormant/lapsed users, high-value buyers, and intent tiers (case: max-fashion.md — campaigns built for "new app users, dormant customers, high-value buyers" using behavior, language, and shopping data; automotive-1.md — 10+ intent segments with segment-specific retargeting; 6thStreet/retail-d2c-7.md — "segmented, contextual and interactive campaigns").
- **Reactivate dormant users with personalized promotions** — a dedicated dormant-segment reactivation message outperforms other channels (case: swiggy.md — dormant-user reactivation saw 1.5X better reactivation rate over other channels).
- **Tie campaigns to events and seasonal moments** — event-anchored campaigns recur across the corpus (case: max-fashion.md — "Suhoor Surprises" in late Ramadan hours, Back to School, White Wednesday; tata-cliq.md — Diwali and Black Friday personalized campaigns drove $500K in one month; retail-d2c-16.md — Ramadan/Eid).

## Conversion & Commerce Campaigns

- **Recover abandoned carts with alerts carrying an embedded promo code** — cart-recovery nudges with an incentive accelerate purchase completion (case: retail-d2c-5.md — "cart recovery alerts with embedded promo codes"; tata-cliq.md — abandoned-cart follow-ups and price-drop alerts on wishlisted products).
- **Recommend products from browsing/purchase history inside the campaign** — behavior-based recommendations drive higher conversion (case: tata-cliq.md — recommendations based on browsing history; housing-com.md — property-recommendation bot personalized via user data and search history, conversion up 80%).
- **For high-ticket products, drive to a store visit rather than a direct online buy** — share a coupon then navigate the user to the nearest store (case: retail-d2c-2.md — high-ticket mattresses/furniture: assessed interest, shared coupon, navigated to nearest store, cut qualified-lead cost 75%; retail-d2c-14.md — premium lens brand shared store-redeemable coupon).
- **Use clear, action-specific CTAs matched to the customer's preferred channel** — e.g. "Shop in-store" vs "Shop online," plus click-to-shop links and store locators (case: max-fashion.md — clear CTAs plus click-to-shop links and store locators increased conversions).

## Contests, Gamification & Brand Engagement

- **Run interactive contests to drive engagement and repeat participation** — QR-to-WhatsApp contest entry and gamified mechanics generate high message volume and return visits (case: mtv.md — QR-code-to-WhatsApp contests: 1.87M messages, 47.3K engaged users, 26% returned; entertainment-3.md — giveaway/contest CTWA ads; retail-d2c-19.md — "Spin & Win" gamification).

## What the Evidence Does NOT Support

The case-study corpus contains no evidence for SMS campaign best practices — every SMS reference is a negative baseline that brands moved away from (e.g. reserva.md: WhatsApp CTR 40% vs 10% on SMS; retail-d2c-9.md: "SMS and emails not performing"; financial-services-5.md: "growing weariness among users towards traditional SMS"). Do not add SMS best-practices content until real SMS case studies exist.
