## Primary Messaging Angles (Use by default)

- Quantified problem ("19 days")
- Specific solution ("audit prevents rejection")
- Emotional trigger (cost + time pressure)

**Conversion rate:** 40–50%  
**Best for segments:** India SMB, Enterprise, Healthcare, Education

## Messaging by Customer Journey Stage

### AWARENESS (First message, unknown prospect)

**Goal:** Get them to engage, not sell yet

**Use angle:**
- "Know your use case first. OTP? Campaigns? Support? Tell us, and we'll guide."
- "Real estate using WhatsApp for property updates. Are you?"
- "Healthcare clinics sending appointment reminders via WhatsApp. How?"

**Don't use:**
- ❌ Product features ("WhatsApp + SMS + RCS")
- ❌ Pricing ("₹2.5K/month")
- ❌ Competitor comparison

---

### CONSIDERATION (They're serious, asking questions)

**Goal:** Show you understand their pain and have a solution

**Use angle:**
- **Compliance/approval:** "19-day template approval frustrating? Audit prevents rejection."
- **DLT:** "DLT confusion? We guide TSP co-registration. Avoid fines."
- **Vertical:** "Real estate teams launch campaigns in 48 hours. Pre-built templates ready."
- **Multi-channel:** "One platform for WhatsApp + SMS + RCS. Single audit trail."

**Don't use:**
- ❌ "We're the best" (unverified)
- ❌ "Faster than Twilio" (can't control Meta approval)
- ❌ Price (wait until they ask)

## Objection Handlers (By Objection)

### Objection: "Why pay ₹2.5K if AiSensy is free?"

**Response (use Angle 7):**
- "AiSensy is genuinely better for <1K msgs/month. It's free, and it works."
- "At 2K+ msgs/month, you'll need compliance + support. That's worth ₹2.5K."
- "Try AiSensy now. When you scale, switch to us. We'll give you a discount."

**Why it works:**
- Acknowledges AiSensy's strength (honesty)
- Sets expectation (growth triggers switch)
- Removes sales pressure (customer feels trusted)

---

### Objection: "Twilio is more flexible / developer-friendly"

**Response (use Angle 4):**

- Positions as different category (not competing)
- Offers coexistence (both can work together)

---

### Objection: "Template approval takes 19 days. Can you speed it up?"

**Response (use Angle 1):**
- "We can't speed Meta's 19-day window (that's on them)."

**Why it works:**
- Honest about limitation (builds credibility)
- Reframes the win (prevent rejection, not speed approval)
- Quantified improvement (5 days saved)

## Do's and Don'ts Summary

### DO:

✓ Lead with their pain, not your product  
✓ Quantify the benefit ("saves 19 days," "₹50K+ avoided")  
✓ Acknowledge competitor strengths ("AiSensy is genuinely free")  
✓ Offer honesty over hype ("We can't speed Meta approval")  
✓ Provide an escape hatch ("AiSensy better for your volume")  
✓ Use specific language from customer transcripts  

### DON'T:

❌ Lead with features ("WhatsApp + SMS + RCS")  
❌ Make unverified claims ("Only platform with compliance audit")  
❌ Oversell capability ("Fastest approval in industry")  
❌ Ignore the objection (answer what they asked, not what you want to say)  
❌ Pretend competitors are bad (they're often good in their niche)  
❌ Use marketing-speak ("Industry-leading," "Best-in-class")

## Testing & Iteration

**How to test these angles:**

1. **Run A/B tests:** Use two angles with otherwise identical campaigns. Measure conversion rate.
2. **Track customer quotes:** When a customer says "This convinced me," note the language.
3. **Iterate:** Keep angles that close deals. Kill angles that don't.
4. **Segment validation:** Test angle by segment. Compliance audit works for Healthcare; DLT works for India SMB.

**What success looks like:**
- Conversations get longer (20+ messages instead of 3–4 abandonment)
- Customers ask specific follow-up questions (high intent signal)
- Customers schedule demos/callbacks (sales signal)
- Conversion rate increases (measure before/after)
