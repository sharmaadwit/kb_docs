# Welcome

Your AI-powered assistant for getting things done
SuperAgent is an AI-powered platform that helps you accomplish real tasks. Chat with an intelligent assistant that can use tools, skills, and integrations to do things for you—from sending emails and analyzing data to scheduling tasks and browsing the web.
What can you do with SuperAgent?
Agents
Create specialized AI assistants for specific jobs—research, writing, data analysis, and more.
Skills
Add custom capabilities like data analysis, email, spreadsheets, and other tools to your agents.
Integrations
Connect Gmail, Jira, Google Sheets, Google Docs, Slack, and other apps so your AI can work with your data.
Scheduled Tasks
Automate recurring work with scheduling and triggers—daily summaries, weekly reports, and more.
Recipes
Use pre-built workflow templates to get started quickly with common tasks.
Browser Control
Let agents browse the web, fill forms, and interact with websites on your behalf.
